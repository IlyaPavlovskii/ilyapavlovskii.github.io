description: "Dice Roller is a fast, offline dice-rolling app for tabletop games, RPGs, and casual play. Roll standard polyhedral dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100), combine multiple dice in a single throw, and review your roll history at a glance. The app works fully offline, doesn't require an account, and is designed to stay out of the way so you can focus on the game."
